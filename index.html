About Mega Zing Spa

Where Eastern Traditions Meet Western Comfort

Established in 2019 and officially opened in 2020, Mega Zing Spa is a welcoming wellness destination located in San Dimas, California.

Conveniently situated in the same shopping plaza as AutoZone, near the intersection of the 57 and 210 freeways, our spa offers easy access and ample parking.

We are dedicated to combining traditional Eastern techniques with modern Western approaches, providing a relaxing and personalized spa experience at affordable prices.

Our Spa Environment

Spacious, comfortable and thoughtfully designed.

Our spacious 2,750-square-foot facility features eight comfortable treatment rooms, including three beautifully decorated couples’ rooms designed to create a warm and romantic atmosphere.

We also offer a dedicated shower room equipped with a professional Table Shower and a separate shower.

Our services include professional massage therapy, beauty treatments, couples massage, body and foot scrubs, and Table Shower treatments.

Every detail of our spa is thoughtfully designed to create a peaceful, welcoming, and comfortable environment.

Our Commitment to Cleanliness & Comfort

Fresh linens and guest comfort come first.

At Mega Zing Spa, cleanliness and guest comfort are among our highest priorities.

All massage and treatment beds are prepared with freshly laundered sheets, pillowcases, towels, and coverings for each guest.

For Table Shower treatments, we use fresh disposable protective liners and replace all linens and washable accessories after every use.

During the colder months, our massage and treatment beds are equipped with electric warming blankets to provide additional warmth and comfort.

We believe a clean and comfortable environment is an essential part of a wonderful spa experience.

Our Professional Team

Experienced professionals with a passion for quality care.

Our dedicated team consists of experienced massage therapists and beauty professionals who hold the appropriate California professional licenses or certifications.

All licensed and certified professionals display their professional credentials while on duty, reflecting our commitment to professionalism, transparency, and guest confidence.

Our team members bring years of practical experience, professional training, and a genuine passion for providing quality care.

By combining traditional Eastern massage techniques with modern Western approaches, we strive to provide personalized treatments tailored to each guest’s individual needs.

We believe that professional spa treatments should be enjoyable, comfortable, and accessible.
